# PRODUCT.md: ScamKill (QuadDraKe)

## Product Truth
- **Name:** ScamKill (*Lá chắn AI giúp người cao tuổi và gia đình cùng chống lừa đảo qua điện thoại*)
- **Team / Organization:** QuadDraKe (Lê Quỳnh Thư, Phạm Quốc Thanh, Nguyễn Khoa Quang Minh, cộng sự | HITC InnoStar 2026)
- **Vision:** *No one has to face a phone scam alone. (Không ai phải đối mặt với lừa đảo một mình.)*
- **Default Language & Bilingual Parity:** Loads in **English (`en`)** by default for international and competition judges, with a 1-tap instantaneous switch to **Vietnamese (`vi`)** across every screen, modal, scenario, guidebook, and island.
- **Core Mechanism:** A 4-Step Family Protection Loop (**1. Detect => 2. Slow Down => 3. Verify => 4. Co-Decide**) spanning the 3 chronological stages of a phone scam:
  1. **Before picking up a call (Layer 1: Call Shield):** Matches caller numbers against ScamKill community reports, public advisories (nTrust/Bộ Công an), and behavioral heuristics (foreign prefixes, burst calls, not in contacts). Honest epistemic boundary: displays `"Unverified / Chưa xác minh"` when data is insufficient rather than guessing.
  2. **While reading a message (Layer 2: Message Analyzer):** Flags impersonation, artificial urgency (*"within 24 hours"*), OTP/money demands, malicious links, and APK lures with 1 to 3 plain-language reasons, 0.85x voice readback, and one clear instruction (*"Do not tap the link. Ask your family first."*).
  3. **At the moment of decision (Layer 3: Family Verification & Multi-Bot SOS):**
     - **Voice-Clone Relative Protocol:** Prompts the senior to (i) ask the pre-agreed **Family Safe-Word (`Mã gia đình`)** and (ii) **hang up and call back** the saved number in their contacts, paired with a 60-second physiological breathing pause and a 48-hour high-risk cooling-off lock.
     - **Consent-Gated Multi-Bot Alert:** 1-tap `"I Am Suspicious / Tôi nghi ngờ"` dispatches alerts via **SMS, Zalo, and Telegram**. Default notifications send only Alert Type, Timestamp, and Phone Number; message or call content is shared only when the senior explicitly consents (PDPD 2025 compliant).

## Behavioral Science & Neuroscience Foundation
1. **Interrupting Amygdala Hijack (Prefrontal Cortex Re-engagement):** Scammers manufacture acute threat ("Your child is in the ER", "Police arrest warrant") to trigger a sympathetic nervous system spike. ScamKill inserts a 60-second **Physiological Box-Breathing Shield** (4s inhale, 4s exhale) that lowers heart rate and re-engages executive reasoning before any banking app is opened.
2. **Miller's Law Under Stress (Cognitive Load Compression):** Acute stress compresses working memory to 2-3 chunks. Every ScamKill result screen caps explanations at 1 to 3 plain sentences and presents a single primary action button (Hick's Law).
3. **Implementation Intentions (Gollwitzer If-Then Protocol):** Pre-committing a concrete rule (*"If a caller asks for urgent money, then I ask our Family Safe-Word first"*) removes the burden of improvising under manipulation.
4. **Polyvagal Co-Regulation:** Bringing a trusted child or grandchild into the loop via 1-tap SMS/Zalo/Telegram breaks the scammer's isolation tactic immediately.
