## 2. Scope & Chronological Protection Layers
1. **Layer 1: Pre-Call Risk Warning (`Tính năng 1: Cảnh báo cuộc gọi từ số có nguy cơ trước khi bắt máy`)**:
   - Caller reputation lookup against community reports (`ScamKill`), public/partner feeds (`nTrust`/`Bộ Công an`), and heuristic signals (unknown international prefix, burst calling, number not in contacts).
   - Honest epistemic boundary: displays risk levels (`high_risk`, `caution`, `unverified` / `"Unverified / Chưa xác minh"`, `safe`) without claiming 100% certainty on unverified numbers.
   - Full-screen Incoming Call Overlay & Simulator (including the canonical **Mrs. Lan / Bà Lan 30M VND accident call** scenario, fake Police/Court impersonation call, and unknown number check).
2. **Layer 2: Suspicious Message Analysis (`Tính năng 2: Phân tích tin nhắn đáng ngờ khi đọc`)**:
   - Multi-Agent Analysis Pipeline (`Intake Agent` -> `Red Flag Agent` -> `Psychological Pressure Agent` -> `RAG Evidence Matcher` -> `Elder Action Assembler`).
   - Detects organization impersonation, time pressure ("within 24 hours"), money/OTP demands, abnormal links/QR codes, and APK/app-install lures.
   - Outputs risk level (`low`/`medium`/`high` mapped to `safe`/`caution`/`high_risk`), 1 to 3 plain-language reasons, and concrete next action ("Do not tap the link. Ask your family first.").
3. **Layer 3: Relative Verification & Family Intervention (`Tính năng 3: Xác minh người thân và báo cho gia đình khi cần quyết định`)**:
   - **4-Step Family Protection Loop (`Vòng bảo vệ gia đình`)**: `1. Detect (Phát hiện)` => `2. Slow Down (Làm chậm / 60s Breathing Pause)` => `3. Verify (Xác minh Mã gia đình & Gọi lại)` => `4. Co-Decide (Cùng quyết định với Gia đình)`.
   - **Mechanism 3A: Multi-Bot Family Alert (`Báo người thân`)**: One-tap `"I Am Suspicious / Tôi nghi ngờ"` or automatic high-risk trigger sends alerts via **SMS, Zalo, and Telegram**. Strict consent gate: default alert sends **only alert type, timestamp, and phone number involved**; raw message or call transcript is shared **only when the senior explicitly consents**.
   - **Mechanism 3B: Voice-Clone & Relative Emergency Verification (`Xác minh giả giọng người thân`)**: Actionable 2-step verification protocol independent of AI audio limits:
     1. Ask the pre-agreed **Family Safe-Word (`Mã gia đình`)**.
     2. **Hang up and call back** using the saved contact number or familiar video call app.
4. **Interactive Annotated Guidebook & Competition Submission Pack**:
   - Interactive Guidebook in both App (`InteractiveGuidebookModal.tsx`) and Landing Page (`InteractiveGuidebook.svelte` + `/guidebook`) with SVG directional arrows, pulsing numbered circles, 3 persona views (Seniors, Family, Judges), and a `1.0x / 1.5x / 2.0x` zoom layer.
   - Full HD screenshots (English and Vietnamese) + automated MP4/WebM demo walkthrough video in `./submissions/` and embedded on the Landing Page.
