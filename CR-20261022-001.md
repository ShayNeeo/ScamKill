## 2. Scope & Chronological Protection Layers
1. **Layer 1 — Pre-Call Risk Warning (`Tính năng 1: Cảnh báo cuộc gọi từ số có nguy cơ — trước khi bắt máy`)**:
   - Caller reputation lookup against community reports (`ScamKill`), public/partner feeds (`nTrust`/`Bộ Công an`), and heuristic signals (unknown international prefix, burst calling, number not in contacts).
   - Honest epistemic boundary: displays risk levels (`high_risk`, `caution`, `unverified` / `"Chưa xác minh"`, `safe` / `"Đã lưu trong danh bạ"`) — never claims 100% certainty on unverified numbers.
   - Full-screen Incoming Call Overlay & Simulator (including the canonical **Bà Lan 30M VND accident call** scenario, fake Police/Court impersonation call, and unknown number check).
2. **Layer 2 — Suspicious Message Analysis (`Tính năng 2: Phân tích tin nhắn đáng ngờ — khi đọc`)**:
   - Multi-Agent Analysis Pipeline (`Intake Agent` -> `Red Flag Agent` -> `Psychological Pressure Agent` -> `RAG Evidence Matcher` -> `Elder Action Assembler`).
   - Detects organization impersonation, time pressure ("trong 24 giờ"), money/OTP demands, abnormal links/QR codes, and APK/app-install lures.
   - Outputs risk level (`low`/`medium`/`high` mapped to `safe`/`caution`/`high_risk`), 1–3 plain-language Vietnamese reasons, and concrete next action ("Đừng bấm liên kết. Hỏi người thân trước").
   - Deterministic instant responses for canonical pitch scenarios + live LLM (`9router` / Google AI Studio) with rule+RAG fallback.
3. **Layer 3 — Relative Verification & Family Intervention (`Tính năng 3: Xác minh người thân và báo cho gia đình — khi cần quyết định`)**:
   - **Vòng bảo vệ gia đình (4-Step Loop)**: `Phát hiện (Detect)` => `Làm chậm (Slow Down / Cooling-Off Pause)` => `Xác minh (Verify Safe-Word & Callback)` => `Cùng quyết định (Co-Decide with Family)`.
   - **Mechanism 3A — Multi-Bot Family Alert (`Báo người thân`)**: One-tap `"Tôi nghi ngờ"` / `"Kiểm tra"` or automatic high-risk trigger sends alerts via **SMS, Zalo, and Telegram**. Strict consent gate: default alert sends **only alert type, timestamp, and phone number involved**; raw message/call transcript is shared **only when the senior explicitly consents**.
   - **Mechanism 3B — Voice-Clone & Relative Emergency Verification (`Xác minh giả giọng người thân`)**: Actionable 2-step verification protocol independent of AI audio limits:
     1. Ask the pre-agreed **Family Safe-Word (`Mã gia đình`)**.
     2. **Hang up and call back** using the saved contact number or familiar video call app.
     Includes simulated AI Voice Deepfake / Script Risk indicator labeled clearly as R&D / assistive signal.
4. **"Fake It Until You Make It" Demo & Multi-Bot Hub**:
   - Floating/accessible **"Kịch bản Demo" (Scenario Drawer)** to trigger canonical scenarios (Bà Lan 30M emergency call, Police Zalo warrant, Fake Bank OTP SMS, Safe family call) in 1 tap.
   - Unified TypeScript Express backend (`server.js`) hosting `/api/analyze`, `/api/call-check`, `/api/family-alert`, `/api/bots/status`, `/api/bots/inbox`, `/api/scenarios`, plus real Telegram Bot API integration (env-driven `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN`, never hardcoded) and simulated/webhook Zalo OA + SMS gateway with a live **Family & Bot Receiver Inbox** inside the app.
